GOVERNANCE

Settings

Roles, status definitions, and the rules the system enforces. Use the Tweaks panel to preview any role.

Roles & access

Leadership — Rose Martinez
Full access · approves matches · sees admin-only notes · escalation owner.
Admin — Dana Whitfield
Verification & review authority · approves matches · manages DNP holds · sees admin-only notes.
Placement Coordinator — Carmen Delgado
Owns needs & placements · prepares matches · works risk queue · cannot approve matches.
Fulfillment — Marcus Lee
Intake, documents & sourcing · works risk queue · cannot approve matches.
Sales Viewer — Kim Sato
Read-only. No admin notes, no internal risk notes, no approvals — supply confidence without sensitive detail.

Placement rules — enforced

01No match is ever auto-approved. Approval requires a named Admin or Leadership decision, logged with reviewer and date.
02Qualifiers on a Do Not Place hold cannot be proposed to any company until the hold is closed by Admin.
03A need must be Approved to Match at admin review before any match can be approved against it.
04Placements longer than 6 months require a backup / on-deck qualifier plan; gaps surface on the dashboard.
05Every license health, risk, or match recommendation carries "Human review required before external use."
06Nothing in QualifierManageOS is legal advice, and no licensing outcome is ever guaranteed.

Review rules — cadence

Qualifier re-verification every 180 days; 30-day queue shows on the dashboard.
Licenses re-verified against state sources at least 90 days before expiration; stale verification (>9 months) flags as Missing Verification.
Placement renewal reviews open 60–90 days before the expected end date; overdue reviews become priority actions.
Document expirations are checked weekly; anything inside 90 days raises an alert.
Every placement gets a review record (reliability · communication · document readiness) at renewal or exit.

Data visibility & sensitive information

Background and credit checks are stored as statuses only — reports and personal identifiers stay with the screening vendor.
Admin-only notes and performance reviews are visible to Admin & Leadership; they never appear in exports or sales views.
Risk resolution notes are internal to CCA staff and hidden from the Sales Viewer role.
Phase 1 is internal-only: no qualifier or company logs in, and nothing is published externally without human review.

Status definitions

QUALIFIER LIFECYCLE
New → Intake Started → Documents Requested → Under Review → Verified → Active (placed). Paused, Do Not Place Pending Review, and Inactive sit outside the happy path and block matching.
LICENSE HEALTH
Verified Current · Renewal Window · Expiring Soon · Expired · Missing Verification · Human Review Required · Do Not Place Pending Review. Only Verified Current, Renewal Window, and Expiring Soon (with a filed renewal) can support a match.
NEED & MATCH FLOW
Draft → Open → Under Review → Match Proposed → Match Approved → Documents Pending → Active. Matches: Suggested / Possible Fit / Best Fit / Backup Only / Not Recommended, each requiring Pending → Approved or Rejected by a human.
RISK LEVELS
Low · Medium · High · Critical. Critical flags escalate to Leadership immediately and freeze related matching until reviewed.
